# Privacy Policy — DREADFALL

**Effective date:** October 13, 2026
**Developer:** Hundred Studios

DREADFALL ("the app") is an offline first-person shooter game for Android. It has a single-player campaign, two-player split-screen versus on one device, and Wi-Fi multiplayer between devices on the same local network.

## Data we collect

**We do not collect, store, or share any personal data.**

- The app has no user accounts, no sign-in, and no registration.
- The app contains no advertising, no in-app purchases, and no analytics, crash-reporting, or tracking SDKs.
- The app does not connect to any server operated by us or by anyone else on the internet. We never receive any information about you or your device.

## Data stored on your device

The following data stays entirely on your device, in the app's private storage, and is never transmitted to us:

- Your campaign progress, unlocked levels, and best results (kills, items, secrets, time) per level.
- Your settings: volume levels, look speed, inverted look, free look, and auto-aim choice.

Uninstalling the app deletes all of this data.

## Wi-Fi multiplayer

Wi-Fi multiplayer is optional and only starts when you choose **Host a Wi-Fi match** or **Join a Wi-Fi match** in the Multiplayer menu.

- The devices in a match talk directly to each other over your local Wi-Fi network (or a phone hotspot). Nothing is routed through our servers, because we do not run any.
- To let others find your match, the host's device announces on the local network that a match is available, and joining devices scan the local network for such announcements. These messages stay on the local network.
- During a match, the devices exchange only the game data needed to play: player positions, movement and actions, and the match score. Players are identified only by generic labels such as "P1" and "P2". No names, accounts, contacts, or other personal information are exchanged.
- Other players on the same network can see the match you host or join, as with any local network game. Only play with people you trust on shared or public Wi-Fi.

Split-screen versus runs entirely on one device and uses no network.

## Permissions

- **Internet / network state / Wi-Fi state / Wi-Fi multicast** — Android requires these for any app that opens network connections, including local network ones. DREADFALL uses them only to find and connect to other devices for local Wi-Fi multiplayer.
- **Vibration** — for optional haptic feedback while playing.

The app does not request access to your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos, files, or calendar.

## Third parties

The app contains no third-party advertising, analytics, or data-collection code. It is built with the open-source Godot game engine, which does not collect data from the app. The game's artwork, sound, and music include assets from the open-source Freedoom project.

## Children

The app is not directed at children under 13. The app does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children.
